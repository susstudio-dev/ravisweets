## 2. Regulatory & compliance (India) — the part most plans get wrong

### 2.1 Liquid nitrogen — **point-of-sale is banned**
FSSAI's **advisory of 03 June 2024** declares point-of-sale / plating use of liquid nitrogen (explicitly naming ice creams, cocktails, **meetha paan**, bakery items and desserts for "theatrical flair") **unauthorized** under the FSS Act 2006. Consumers have been injured (frostbite, burns, internal-organ damage). Appendix C permits LN₂ **only** as a processing aid (contact freezing/cooling) during manufacture — **not** for immediate consumption.

**What this means for us:**
- **No "dragon's breath" / smoke-at-the-table.** Ever. Not in store, not at events.
- Piece 05 (Paan Cloud) uses nitro/blast freezing **back-of-house only**, and **all nitrogen must fully sublimate before the piece is served or boxed.** It reaches the customer as an ordinary frozen petit-four.
- This is non-negotiable for a brand built on trust. Lead with "FSSAI-safe craft" as a *positive* differentiator vs reckless dessert-bars.

### 2.2 Edible gold & silver (vark)
FSSAI (2016) **banned animal-origin material** in silver-leaf manufacturing. Use only **certified vegetarian vark** (machine-beaten on polyester / calcium-coated sheets) and **food-grade 24K gold (E175)**, both COA-backed.
- Source from reputable vark vendors who can produce the FSSAI-compliance + vegetarian certificate.
- **Market it:** "100% vegetarian vark" is a genuine trust badge in India — print it on the box.

### 2.3 Probiotic / functional claims (Living Rabri, piece 10)
- Sold as a **"cultured-milk sweet"** with **no health/disease claim** → covered by the standard FSSAI manufacturing licence.
- If we ever want to print "probiotic" / "gut-friendly" front-of-pack, that's a **Nutraceutical / Health-Supplement product approval** under FSS (Health Supplements, Nutraceuticals…) Regulations 2016 — separate filing (~30–45 days, ~₹35K), needs NABL evidence and live-culture CFU counts. **Phase 2** — launch without the claim.

### 2.4 FSSAI licence
- Ravi Sweets already operates under an FSSAI manufacturing licence (Khammam + Kondapur). Confirm the **product categories** on the licence cover confectionery / dairy-based sweets / chocolate. Add categories if missing (amendment, ~7–15 days).
- **Labelling (every box + every piece sleeve):** ingredients (descending order), allergen declaration (nuts, dairy, gluten, soy), net weight, MRP (incl. all taxes), mfg date, **best-before / use-by**, FSSAI licence number, **veg/non-veg mark** (green dot — everything here is veg), batch/lot number, mfr name + address, and nutritional information panel.

### 2.5 NABL testing
- Run **NABL-accredited shelf-life + nutrition panels** on each of the 7 shelf-stable pieces before launch (~₹6K/piece, 14-day turnaround). Stamp a real "best within" date — don't guess.

## 4. Pricing, GST & unit economics

### 4.1 GST — the detail that quietly eats margin
- **Traditional mithai / sweetmeats (HSN 2106 90):** **5% GST.**
- **Chocolate & chocolate-based products (HSN 1806):** **18% GST.**
- Pieces **08 (Gongura Chilli Bonbon)** and **09 (Bidri Bar)** are chocolate-based → **18%**. The other eight are mithai → 5%.
- **Handling:** because the box is a mixed supply sold at a single price, treat it under composite/mixed-supply rules — to be conservative and audit-clean, the simplest path is to **bill the box at the highest applicable rate (18%)** or itemise. Confirm with the brand's CA before Drop 01. The ₹2,400 price is **MRP inclusive of GST** either way; the GST choice affects *our* net, not the customer's price.

- QC: every box hand-checked; the run-number etch is the final QC gate (a box only gets numbered once it passes).

## 6. Channels & fulfilment (India reality)

| Channel | Fit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **D2C site** (`/vault-10`) | **Primary** | Already built; add the drop + live counter page |
| **Hyderabad store pickup** | **Primary for fresh-three** | Drives footfall + lets us serve all 10 |
| **Events / pop-ups** | High | Pop-up at a Hyderabad lifestyle venue = the "fresh three" experience + content |
| **Corporate gifting** | Medium | A numbered box is a *stunning* premium client gift; sell in 25+ lots |
| **Quick-commerce (Zepto/Blinkit)** | **No** | Wrong context for a ₹2,400 collector box; keep core range there, not this |

### 6.1 Payments
- **Razorpay** — UPI is dominant in India (GPay/PhonePe/Paytm), then cards, then netbanking. (Razorpay integration is a separate workstream — see the brand's payment-integration thread.)
- **Disable COD for the Vault 10.** A ₹2,400 premium collector box has high RTO (return-to-origin) risk and refused-delivery losses on COD. Prepaid only — which is fine, because drop buyers are intent-driven.

### 6.2 Logistics & cold chain
- **SS boxes:** Delhivery / Bluedart / Shiprocket-aggregated; nationwide; 2–5 day.
- **Cold-chain metro boxes:** gel-pack + insulated liner; dispatch **only Tue–Thu** to avoid weekend warehouse hold; metro pin-codes only (Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Chennai, Mumbai, Pune, Delhi-NCR, Bangalore); 48h SLA; clear "eat within 5–7 days" messaging.
- **Hyderabad local:** Porter / Dunzo / own rider for same-day fresh-three.
- Publish a **pin-code check** at checkout: cold-chain pins get the all-10 box; the rest get the SS-seven box + Hyderabad fresh-three claim card.

## 7. Marketing plan (India-specific)

### 7.1 The wedge (web-validated, May 2026)
- **Bombay Sweet Shop** does limited-edition *flavour* collections + Parle-G collabs — but not serialised molecular collector drops.
- **Khoya Mithai** has Le Cordon Bleu chefs + premium gifting — but classic-format mithai, no technique theatre, no numbered scarcity.
- **No premium mithai house runs a numbered, sneaker-style drop of technique-driven single-bite sweets.** That's the lane.
- Benchmarks: limited drops earn **3–10× revenue/product**; capped quantity + waitlist FOMO justifies premium pricing (Shopify / Queue-it); micro/mid influencer **unboxing Reels** have driven **25M+ views / ~300% traffic spikes** for small Indian D2C brands.

### 7.2 The 3-act drop engine

**Act 1 · Whisper (W-4 → W-1)**
- Blurred macro teasers — "10 sweets that don't exist yet" — one cryptic frame/day on Reels + Shorts.
- Open the `/vault-10` waitlist with a live "500 boxes only" counter.
- Tease **one grail piece** (e.g. Qubani Caviar) in a 15s slow-mo reel.
- WhatsApp broadcast + email capture; seed Hyderabad WhatsApp foodie groups + NRI community lists.

**Act 2 · Drop (W0)**
- Master 60s drop trailer across Reels / Shorts / YouTube.
- Waitlist gets a **24h early-access link**, then public sale.
- Live sold-out counter + a "you got box 287/500" share card auto-generated on every order.
- **6 seeded creators** post embargoed unboxings at drop hour.

**Act 3 · Proof (W1 → W3)**
- Publish the sell-out: "Drop 01 gone in __ hours" — the headline that fuels Drop 02.
- Repost buyer UGC; run a "which piece broke the internet" poll.
- Pitch press (food + design + startup desks) with the craft + numbers angle.
- Open Drop 02 waitlist (swap 2–3 pieces) → compounding list.

## 9. Risks & mitigations (India)

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| FSSAI point-of-sale LN₂ ban | Back-of-house freeze only; full sublimation before serving; no smoke theatre. Lead with "FSSAI-safe craft." |
| Cold-chain breakage on fresh pieces | Two-tier box; metro-only cold-chain; Tue–Thu dispatch; gel-pack + 48h SLA; clear use-by. |
| GST mis-classification (chocolate 18% vs mithai 5%) | CA sign-off before Drop 01; bill mixed box conservatively; itemise if audited. |
| Reads as gimmick not craft | Every piece traces to a real technique + heritage cue; content leads with the maker + the why. |
| Single-supplier failure (vark/gold/couverture) | Dual-source 70/30 from day one with COAs on file. |
| Shelf-life claim risk (no preservatives) | NABL-test every SS piece; stamp real best-before; fresh pieces sold "eat within 48h" by design. |
